Bot's General Information & Purpose

The PlantWithMe Bot is designed to make Forest App sessions easier to share, manage, and track inside Discord communities[cite: 4]. It helps users host focus sessions, invite others to join, keep track of active plant timers, and view finished-session stats and leaderboards[cite: 4].

PlantWithMe is an independent, community-built Discord bot and is not officially affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the official Forest App team.
